Meet the gang

Har janwar ki apni jagah

Each animal blocks a different shape around it. Same simple rules, but every animal makes you think in a new way.

Light stripes = its row and column. Those are already blocked by rule 1 and 2. The coloured cells are the personal space. On a mixed board both animals' spaces count.

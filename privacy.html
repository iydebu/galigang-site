Privacy policy

For the Gali Gang app on Android. Last updated: 5 October 2026.

Gali Gang is a puzzle game made by Devashish Tiwari (Debu). This page says what data the game uses and why. In short: the game itself does not collect your personal data. It only sends something to us when you send a bug report yourself. The ads in the game come from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data to show them.

What the game keeps

  • Your progress, coins, settings and stats are saved only on your phone. They are not sent to us.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email or location.
  • If you turn on reminders, the game sets a local notification on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server for this.

Bug reports

In Settings you can press Report a bug. Nothing is sent unless you write a report and press Send. Then the game sends:

  • the text you wrote and the type you picked (for example "Bug" or "Idea"),
  • a picture of the game screen that was open behind Settings,
  • the game's recent log (what the game did in the last minutes; it has no personal data),
  • the game version, the level you were on, and your phone model, Android version, memory and screen size.

It does not send your name, contacts, location or advertising ID. The report goes to a private Discord channel that only the Gali Gang team can read, and is used only to fix the game. A copy is also saved on your phone. Do not write private details in the report.

Update check

When the game starts it reads a small file from galigang.iydebu.com to see if a newer version is out. Like any web request this shows your IP address to the website host. The game sends nothing else.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • the advertising ID of your device,
  • your IP address and rough location (city or country level),
  • device information such as model and Android version,
  • how you interact with the ads.

In the EU, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before ads use your data for personalised ads. You can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Read how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Gali Gang is a general audience game. It is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Delete your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android settings. This removes everything the game saved on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
